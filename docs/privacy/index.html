Privacy Policy

AURYOND Fitness Adventure — effective September 26, 2026

This policy explains what information the AURYOND Fitness Adventure app ("the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and your choices. The app is a fitness quest game: you complete small workout quests and we track your progress so it survives across devices and sessions.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — the email address you register with, and any display name or avatar image you choose to set.
  • Game progress — quest and workout completions, XP, levels, streaks, achievements, cosmetics, and settings. This is gameplay data required for the app to function.
  • No precise location, contacts, or health-identifier data. Step counts and workouts are entered or followed on-screen; the app does not read them from system health services.

2. How information is used

  • To provide, sync, and restore your game progress.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To display your chosen profile within the app.

3. What we do not do

  • No analytics or advertising SDKs. The app ships with no third-party trackers, ad networks, or profiling tools.
  • No sale or sharing of your data with third parties for marketing or advertising.
  • Diagnostic logging is local to your device only and is never uploaded.

4. Where data is stored

Account and progress data are stored server-side in our backend (Supabase). Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access is limited to the application and its operators.

5. Retention and Deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time directly in the app: Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, progress, and all associated data — it cannot be undone. You can also request deletion by contacting us (below). Deleting your account removes your progress.

6. Children

The app is a general-audience fitness game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
